REAL RESULT — BRAND & HOMEPAGE DIRECTION

Business identity: Real Result is a marketing company.
Primary promise of the work: help customers discover, understand, and choose the business; connect the follow-up that supports continued growth.
Main line: Get noticed. Get chosen. Keep growing.
Hero hook: Your next customer needs a reason to choose you.
Primary CTA: Let’s Talk Growth
Calling and messaging CTA: Plan My Campaign
Development CTA: Discuss My Project

THE HOMEPAGE EMPHASIS
Lead with customer acquisition, marketing effectiveness, conversion, and follow-up. All three hero slides concern marketing and customer communication. Keep the service catalogue to six short cards. Websites, apps, and software get one supporting card. Detailed scope stays on the relevant service pages.

THE SIX HOMEPAGE CARDS
1. SEO & AI Search
2. Paid Ads & Lead Generation
3. Social Media, Content & Branding
4. Voice, AI Calling & Messaging
5. Conversion & Customer Retention
6. Websites, Apps & Software

VISUAL DIRECTION
Hero 1: Show a coherent campaign and landing-page example. Do not display invented performance figures.
Hero 2: Show the visitor journey from ad to enquiry, with a simple real or clearly labelled concept interface.
Hero 3: Show a voice waveform and SMS/RCS/WhatsApp message examples with generic fictional contact details, labelled as examples where needed.
Use actual work for the proof section. Keep headlines as live text, not embedded in images.

TONE
Use familiar business problems, short sentences, and practical answers. Keep curiosity relevant to the service. State what Real Result does immediately below the hook. A page should answer the visitor’s question before inviting contact.

COMMON PAINS AND DESIRES
Invisible offer -> discovery by relevant buyers.
Clicks without enquiries -> clearer reasons to act.
Weak follow-up -> more useful customer conversations.
One-time buyers -> relevant reasons to return.
Unclear reporting -> better evidence for decisions.
Disconnected tools -> a smoother journey behind the marketing.

No claim is made that every industry has the same buying journey. These are broad starting points to adapt during discovery.
