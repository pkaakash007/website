ARTICLE
Category: Getting Chosen
H1: Your next enquiry is only the beginning.
Introduction: Someone has found you and filled in the form. What happens next deserves as much attention as the campaign that brought them there.

1. WHERE DOES THE ENQUIRY GO?
Does it reach the right person, or wait in a shared inbox? Give each enquiry a clear owner and make its status visible.

2. WHAT DOES THE CUSTOMER SEE?
After submitting the form, does the visitor know it worked? Confirm receipt and explain the next step in plain language.

3. WHAT DOES YOUR TEAM KNOW?
Can they see what the person asked about and where the enquiry came from? Useful context makes the first conversation easier to prepare for.

4. WHAT HAPPENS IF THEY ARE NOT READY?
Some people need more information or more time. Decide what helpful follow-up looks like and respect their communication preferences.

5. WHAT CAN YOU LEARN FROM THE OUTCOME?
Connect the enquiry with its eventual status. Was it suitable? Did a conversation happen? What question or obstacle kept coming up?

YOUR NEXT STEP
Choose one recent enquiry and follow its journey from the form to the outcome. Note where a person had to chase, guess, or repeat information. That is a useful place to investigate.
