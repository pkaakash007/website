TECHNICAL SETUP ON THE REAL DOMAIN
1. Replace the placeholder origin in canonical tags, schema templates, and the sitemap with the confirmed HTTPS domain. No Real Result domain has been guessed.
2. Use one canonical URL for each intended indexable page; align internal links, canonicals, and sitemap URLs. Choose trailing-slash behaviour consistently.
3. Public indexable pages should return the correct content with a successful status. Serve the 404 design with HTTP 404. Keep confirmation and unfinished template pages out of the sitemap and noindex them if accessible.
4. Keep private staging behind authentication or proper noindex handling. Do not publish placeholder proof or business-policy pages.
5. Ensure hosting/CDN rules permit the intended search crawlers. Review crawler identities and current provider documentation before setting AI search or training controls; a training crawler and a search crawler may have different purposes.
6. Use descriptive link text and image alt text based on actual imagery. Verify mobile readability, form behaviour, HTTPS, page performance, and keyboard use on the finished site.
7. Validate structured data and inspect the rendered pages using the search engine tools for the actual site. Templates alone do not complete this step.

EDITING RULES
Do not add repetitive city pages without a real service-area strategy and useful local information. Do not publish a second page targeting the same service under a different name merely to repeat keywords. The intent map is an editorial proposal, not keyword-volume research. Confirm regional language and demand when the market is known.

MEASUREMENT PLAN
Track enquiry submissions, qualified enquiries, completed sales conversations, and meaningful campaign outcomes. Use website analytics and search performance reports available for the property; examine identifiable AI referral traffic where available. Keep reported visibility separate from business outcomes. Do not claim every impression or visit can be attributed precisely.
